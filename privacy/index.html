Privacy notice

Trade Arts respects your privacy. This notice explains the information our website may collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information you choose to send us

When you submit a contact or shop enquiry, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, company and message. We use that information to respond to your enquiry, prepare project discussions or quotes, and arrange product availability, delivery and payment where relevant.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics and Google Ads measurement to understand how people find and use this website and to assess advertising performance. These services may process device, browser, approximate location and interaction information. You can limit this processing through your browser settings and Google’s advertising controls.

Service providers

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Form enquiries are transmitted through FormSubmit and delivered to our email account. Google provides our analytics and advertising measurement. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry records only for as long as they are useful for responding, maintaining business records, or meeting applicable legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Your choices

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete information we no longer need. Contact info@tradearts.work. If you have a privacy concern, please contact us first so we can investigate and respond.

Updates

We may update this notice when our website or service providers change. Last updated 21 August 2026.
