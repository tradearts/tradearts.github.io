Two ways to work together

Bring the design. Or bring the problem.

The starting point changes, but the aim is the same: a buildable scope, a resolved physical outcome and clear responsibility.

01 / Supplied design

Fabricate and finish a resolved design.

Bring drawings, CAD or established creative direction. Trade Arts reviews buildability and scope, then fabricates, finishes and prepares the work for the agreed handover.

02 / End to end

Resolve and deliver an ambitious concept.

Bring a sketch, reference or incomplete design. Trade Arts can develop a practical path through design resolution, prototyping, fabrication, finish and specialist coordination.

Integrated capability

One physical outcome, fewer handoffs.

Capability is organised around the object and its use, not a list of machines.

Resolve

Turn creative intent, references and constraints into a buildable scope.

Fabricate

Combine appropriate digital and hands-on processes around the needs of the object.

Finish

Develop surfaces for the intended viewing distance, lighting, camera or environment.

Deliver

Plan the agreed handover, freight, site coordination and specialist interfaces.

Start with what you have

What needs to be made?

Share the intended use, location, timing and design information you already have. A sketch, reference, drawing or controlled-access CAD link is enough to start.
