Services

Design-to-fabrication support.

Trade Arts works across practical design resolution, digital fabrication, hands-on making and finish. The mix changes with the object, its use and the information you bring.

01

Design resolution and CAD

Develop incomplete ideas into a practical fabrication scope, or prepare and refine supplied design information for making. Required client, engineering and regulatory approvals are identified separately.

02

Prototyping and digital fabrication

Use scanning, modelling, additive or subtractive processes where they suit the form, stage and finish. Process, material, tolerance and scale are agreed for each project.

03

Physical fabrication

Combine workshop processes and specialist suppliers around the needs of the build, with a clear scope and agreed review points.

04

Scenic finish and paintwork

Develop surface, colour and texture for the intended viewing distance, lighting, camera or display context.

05

Exhibition and public-facing work

Support practical design development, fabrication planning, making and handover for exhibits and public-facing objects.

06

Handover and specialist coordination

Plan collection, freight, site interfaces and specialist inputs as part of the agreed project scope.

Specialist boundaryWhere a project requires structural engineering, certification, rigging approval, electrical certification or specialist installation sign-off, those services must be provided or verified by appropriately qualified consultants and contractors within an agreed scope.
